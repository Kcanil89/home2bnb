Who we are

Rachel Lam

Founder / Managing Director

I love anything related to homes and properties! From investing, renovating, building, styling and interior design, management — I have experienced all of it through my own portfolio of properties through the years. I have a keen eye on quality designs, furnishings and home styling.

Having worked in the Financial Service industry for 10 years, I strongly believe in delivering excellent customer satisfaction and service to my business partners and clients to ensure long standing relationships built on trust and commitment.

From all that I have experienced, I have seen an opportunity in the market where property investors should be and can be getting better returns from their investment property. This is why I created HOME2BNB combining my passion for homes and my ability to partner with clients to achieve successful outcomes.

And if the above wasn’t enough, I have 2 adorable boys and my husband Shaun who always keeps me on my toes.

Shaun Sia

Co-Founder / Director

My career started over 17 years ago in professional services as a Financial Auditor. Since then, I have worked in various roles and for a range of clients around the world starting from small local companies to large multinationals.

Shortly into my working career I bought my first investment property. Without any real experience and much to the surprise of family and friends, I hands on endeavored on a top-to-bottom renovation. That experience was one of the most hair pulling yet fulfilling challenges in my life. This was the step that ignited my passion for properties and development. Since then I have renovated more homes, built new and developed blocks in the Perth Metro area.

From all my successful encounters, I have taken away one key lesson — trust is king. Trust in someone is based on their ability to demonstrate they genuinely care about your success.

My wife and I founded this company from our passion for property, our genuine focus on our clients’ success and our belief that Perth is a beautiful place to come and stay a while.
