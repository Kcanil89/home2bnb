Why we exist

Born from boutique hospitality

Founder Sarah Mitchell spent years running guest experience teams for luxury hotels before creating Home2BNB. Her vision was simple: bridge the gap between the warmth of a home and the polish of a five-star stay.

Every residence we manage is treated like a flagship property. We curate interiors, orchestrate operations and obsess over the guest journey to produce rave reviews and reliable returns.

Our pillars

  • Design that balances beauty and durability
  • High-touch service for guests and owners
  • Data-led pricing and forecasting
  • Local partnerships for exceptional experiences
Milestones

The journey so far

From a single coastal retreat to a curated portfolio of design-led homes across WA.

2017

Home2BNB launches with a Fremantle heritage loft and a promise to deliver hotel-level service in a residential setting.

2019

Portfolio expands to ten coastal and wine-region residences, introducing bespoke guest itineraries and concierge services.

2021

Launch of our owner portal with live performance dashboards, maintenance tracking and revenue benchmarks.

2023

Named a top independent property manager in Western Australia for guest satisfaction and revenue growth.
