Our story

Crafting human-centred stays with investor discipline.

Home2BNB was founded by property lovers who saw a gap between investment-grade performance and truly thoughtful hospitality. We believe both should exist together.

Why we began

A boutique team for owners who expect more.

Rachel and Shaun built Home2BNB after years of renovating, styling and managing their own portfolio across Perth. They knew owners wanted better guidance, stronger revenue and a guest experience that inspires loyalty.

Every property we manage receives an intentionally small team—strategist, guest experience lead and housekeeping partner—who become guardians of the asset.

Independent, owner-led business grounded in transparency.
Design-led approach combining form, function and revenue optimisation.
Hands-on hospitality culture obsessed with delighting guests.
