FAQs

Answers to the questions we hear most

We believe informed owners make the best partners. Explore the details of how we operate and what to expect.

Getting started

Onboarding & revenue

How quickly can my property go live?

Most homes launch within 3–4 weeks. We coordinate styling, photography, listing production and distribution while ensuring compliance requirements are met.

Do you provide a revenue projection?

Yes. Our appraisal models factor in seasonality, competition, occupancy bands and rate strategy. We review assumptions together so you’re confident in the numbers.

What if my property needs furnishings?

We offer turnkey styling packages through trusted partners. From soft furnishings to artwork, we manage procurement and installation.

Operations

Guest care & maintenance

Who handles guest communication?

Our in-house guest services team manages messaging before, during and after stays. We’re reachable 24/7 for urgent matters.

How do you protect my property?

We combine ID verification, deposits, screening and house rules with regular inspections. Maintenance issues are triaged immediately.

Can I reserve the property for personal use?

Absolutely. Owners can block dates anytime through our portal or by emailing the team. We’ll coordinate cleaning before and after your stay.

Financials

Fees, reporting & statements

What fees do you charge?

Our management fee ranges from 18–22% of booking revenue depending on property type and service scope. We keep pricing simple and aligned with results.

How are payouts managed?

Owners receive monthly statements with itemised revenue, expenses and payouts. Funds are transferred directly into your nominated account.

Do you help with regulatory compliance?

Yes. We manage licensing requirements, short-stay registrations, insurance verification and safety checks so you remain compliant.

We’re happy to chat through unique scenarios or provide deeper detail on our processes.
