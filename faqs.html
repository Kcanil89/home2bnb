Owner FAQs

All the details on working with Home2BNB

How quickly can we launch once we sign on?

Most homes go live within four to six weeks depending on styling requirements. We’ll map a detailed launch schedule during onboarding so you can follow every milestone.

Do you handle furnishing and procurement?

Yes. Our styling team designs mood boards, sources furnishings and coordinates delivery. We’ll work within your budget to achieve a cohesive, durable look.

Which platforms will feature my property?

We list across Airbnb, Booking.com, Stayz and luxury booking networks as appropriate. Our direct booking engine is also available for repeat guests.

How do you price stays?

We combine market data, competitor benchmarking and seasonal insights to set optimal nightly rates. Rates are updated weekly and major events trigger real-time adjustments.

What does your fee include?

Our management fee covers marketing, guest communications, revenue management, maintenance coordination and reporting. Styling updates and major repairs are scoped separately.

Can I stay in my own property?

Absolutely. We reserve owner stays through our portal so you can plan personal visits without disrupting guest bookings.

Guest FAQs

Supporting exceptional stays from enquiry to checkout

What’s included in the Home2BNB welcome?

Guests receive a personalised digital guidebook, curated welcome gifts, premium linens and on-call concierge assistance throughout their stay.

How do you handle after-hours requests?

Our team monitors communications 24/7. Whether it’s late check-in support or concierge recommendations, we respond within minutes.

Do you allow pets or special events?

Many homes welcome small pets and intimate gatherings. We review each request individually to protect the property and ensure a great experience.

What if something goes wrong during a stay?

Preventative maintenance keeps surprises rare, but if anything happens our operations team coordinates repairs immediately and supports guests with alternatives.
