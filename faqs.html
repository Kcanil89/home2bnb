FAQ

Answers to the questions we hear most.

From onboarding to owner reporting, these are the details that help you decide if Home2BNB is the right fit.

Management

Working with Home2BNB.

Understand how we partner with owners and what you can expect day to day.

How quickly can we launch once we sign?

Most properties go live within four to six weeks. Styling, photography and compliance are scheduled immediately so we can launch with confidence.

What makes your guest experience different?

We offer concierge-level service—pre-arrival planning, curated local experiences and on-call support. It’s how we maintain 97% average review scores.

Do I get to approve bookings?

Yes. You choose your involvement level. Many owners trust us to screen and approve guests, while others prefer to review every reservation via the owner portal.

Financials

Revenue and reporting.

We make sure the numbers are transparent and accessible, always.

How are payouts handled?

Owner payouts are processed monthly with a full statement outlining revenue, expenses and performance commentary. You’ll also see live data inside the portal.

Do you charge any onboarding fees?

We provide a customised onboarding plan with transparent costs for styling, photography and setup. Our management fee is performance-aligned with no hidden extras.

How do you handle taxes and compliance?

We partner with local experts to ensure GST, council registrations and insurance are up to date. We’ll guide you through the paperwork so nothing is missed.
